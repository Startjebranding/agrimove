Why AgriMove

Engineered for the Demands of Professional Horticulture

Every detail of the AgriMove trailer has been designed with one goal: making your operation faster, safer, and more efficient — day after day.

  • All-Terrain Performance

    Six independently supported wheels with robust pneumatic tyres. Handles gravel paths, soft soil, and unpaved nursery floors without hesitation.

  • Lightweight Aluminum Frame

    Aircraft-grade aluminum construction delivers maximum strength with minimum weight — protecting your team and extending product lifespan.

  • Danish Cart Compatible

    Designed around the standard CC container (Danish cart) dimensions. Load and unload effortlessly with the integrated ramp system.

  • Time-Saving Efficiency

    Transport multiple carts in a single run. Reduce manual handling, lower labour costs, and keep your workflow moving at full speed.

  • Low Maintenance

    Corrosion-resistant materials and a minimal-parts design mean less downtime, fewer replacements, and lower total cost of ownership.

  • International Standard

    Compliant with European transport regulations and designed for global export. AgriMove is the professional choice for nurseries worldwide.
